For **this S4 synthetic sample's current G5 acceptance only**, the approved G4 interface accepts a well-formed `POST /api/datasets` by the declarative `ANALYST` actor, with a required positive `Content-Length`, `text/csv` media type, and dataset ID. A request of at most **65,536 raw body bytes** that passes those route, role, and transport checks reaches G2 business validation. An oversized request that reaches the length gate receives HTTP **413 / `HTTP_PAYLOAD_TOO_LARGE`**; its otherwise valid numeric content is not claimed to have been checked under G2. Earlier route or role failures retain their own response precedence. The original G2 kWh grammar has no digit ceiling and remains unchanged in its source document.

The sample exposes no edit or delete route. Unsupported `PUT`, `PATCH`, and `DELETE` calls return HTTP **405 / `METHOD_NOT_ALLOWED`** without state change. This is the accepted result for actions that the current synthetic service does not expose. The G2 business response codes **`MUTATION_NOT_PERMITTED`** and **`IMMUTABLE_HISTORY`** remain **unimplemented**, and this page makes no claim that they were observed.

The 30% criterion, original G2 document, no-login and unproven identity limits, existing service topology, and synthetic-only scope remain unchanged. This clarification does not approve the separately proposed 70% threshold, G6, real business use, or Factory Control adoption. Independent reviews passed the bounded G5 candidate; formal G5 completion also requires exact output sources and reviewer provenance to be registered in Control and then independently checked. The current active Control runtime cannot accept those new sources, so no SATISFIED completion is claimed here.
